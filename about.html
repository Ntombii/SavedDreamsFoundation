ABOUT US

Creating Hope Through Community Support

Founded to bridge the gap in community support, Saved Dreams Foundation works tirelessly to aid vulnerable individuals and disadvantaged communities.

The organisation focuses on supporting disadvantaged school children and the community as a whole. It also advocates for sexual and reproductive health, gender-based violence awareness, mental-health awareness and the prevention of teenage pregnancies.

Saved Dreams Foundation works towards eradicating period poverty and creating a world where women and girls can experience menstrual dignity.

Its mission focuses on normalising menstruation, challenging myths and stigma surrounding menstruation, and empowering girls with the resources they need to manage their periods while continuing to focus on their education and personal goals.

The organisation's activities include problem identification, training, community sensitisation and follow-up visits.

Mission & Vision

Our work is guided by a commitment to support, empower and strengthen communities.

To provide holistic psychosocial support, youth empowerment programmes, family strengthening services and preventative education initiatives that promote wellbeing, resilience, safety and positive life outcomes for children, families and communities.

Every contribution is used to help children cope with the war and heal from the trauma it causes. We work to ensure resources are used responsibly and reach children directly through local partnerships and long term support systems. We are committed to careful stewardship of every gift.

Donations to the Saved Dreams Foundation (or Saved Dreams NPO) are not automatically tax-deductible in South Africa unless the organization holds official Section 18A status approved by the South African Revenue Service (SARS) Online donations are receipted immediately and other donation type receipts are mailed within a week.

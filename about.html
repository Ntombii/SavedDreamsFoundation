About Saved Dreams Foundation

Founded to bridge the gap in community support, Saved Dreams Foundation works tirelessly to aid vulnerable individuals.A non-profit organisation whose work focuses on supporting disadvantaged school children and the community as a whole. The organisation also advocates for sexual and reproductive health, gender-based violence awareness, mental-health awareness and the prevention of teenage pregnancies. The organisation's stated vision is to eradicate period poverty and create a world where women have menstrual dignity. Its mission focuses on normalising menstruation and challenging myths and stigma surrounding menstruation. Its goal is to empower girls with the resources they need to manage their periods so that they can continue focusing on their education and personal goals. The organisation's activities include problem identification, training, community sensitisation and follow-up visits.

Mission & Vision

Mission: To provide holistic communities holistic psychosocial support, youth empowerment programmes, family strengthening services and preventative education initiatives that promote wellbeing, resilience, safety and positive life outcomes for children, families and communities

Vision: To build resilient learners, empowered educators and thriving communities where every child has the opportunity, support and confidence to reach their full potential.

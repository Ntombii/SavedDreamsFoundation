ABOUT US

Creating Hope Through Community Support

Founded to bridge the gap in community support, Saved Dreams Foundation works tirelessly to aid vulnerable individuals and disadvantaged communities.

The organisation focuses on supporting disadvantaged school children and the community as a whole. It also advocates for sexual and reproductive health, gender-based violence awareness, mental-health awareness and the prevention of teenage pregnancies.

Saved Dreams Foundation works towards eradicating period poverty and creating a world where women and girls can experience menstrual dignity.

Its mission focuses on normalising menstruation, challenging myths and stigma surrounding menstruation, and empowering girls with the resources they need to manage their periods while continuing to focus on their education and personal goals.

The organisation's activities include problem identification, training, community sensitisation and follow-up visits.

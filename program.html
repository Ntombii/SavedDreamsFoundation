OUR COMMUNITY WORK

Making a Difference Through Community Programmes

Saved Dreams Foundation develops and supports programmes that respond to the needs of vulnerable children, young people, women and families. Our work focuses on education, empowerment, health awareness, community support and personal development.

Menstrual Dignity & Period Poverty

We work to challenge menstrual stigma and support women and girls affected by period poverty. Our programmes promote menstrual dignity, education and access to information.

  • Menstrual health education
  • Period poverty awareness
  • Menstrual dignity initiatives
  • Community sensitisation
